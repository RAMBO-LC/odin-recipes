Lasagna

Description

Lasagna is a classic Italian dish featuring layers of pasta sheets, rich meat sauce, and creamy béchamel or ricotta cheese. This comforting baked pasta dish is perfect for family dinners and special occasions.

This traditional recipe combines tender pasta layers with a savory meat sauce made from ground beef and tomatoes, creating a delicious and satisfying meal that is sure to impress your guests.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 28 oz canned crushed tomatoes
  • 6 oz tomato paste
  • 2 cups ricotta cheese
  • 2 cups mozzarella cheese, shredded
  • 1/2 cup parmesan cheese, grated
  • 1 egg
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 1 lb lasagna noodles
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Cook lasagna noodles according to package directions. Drain and set aside.
  3. In a large pot, heat olive oil and sauté diced onion until softened.
  4. Add minced garlic and cook for 1 minute until fragrant.
  5. Add ground beef and cook until browned, breaking it up as it cooks.
  6. Stir in crushed tomatoes and tomato paste. Simmer for 20 minutes.
  7. In a bowl, combine ricotta, mozzarella, parmesan, egg, salt, and pepper.
  8. Spread a thin layer of meat sauce on the bottom of a 9x13 baking dish.
  9. Layer noodles, then ricotta mixture, then meat sauce. Repeat layers until all ingredients are used.
  10. End with a layer of sauce and top with remaining mozzarella.
  11. Cover with foil and bake for 25 minutes. Remove foil and bake for an additional 15 minutes until cheese is bubbly.
  12. Let rest for 10 minutes before serving.
